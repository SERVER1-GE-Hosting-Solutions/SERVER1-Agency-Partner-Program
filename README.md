> Infrastructure partnership for SEO, Web Development, Digital Marketing, eCommerce and Software Development agencies.

SERVER1 Agency Partner Program is designed for agencies and technology companies that want a reliable infrastructure partner behind their client projects.

This is not a traditional affiliate program.

The goal is simple:

**You manage growth, development and the client relationship - SERVER1 manages infrastructure.**

Partners can earn recurring commission, request custom infrastructure, coordinate migrations and technical planning with SERVER1, access supported services through API where applicable, protect referred client relationships, and become eligible for relevant opportunities referred by SERVER1.

# Partnership Models

SERVER1 supports two main partnership models.

## 1. Referral Partner

The Partner refers a customer to SERVER1.

SERVER1:

- bills the customer
- provides the infrastructure service
- manages service delivery and support
- attributes the customer to the Partner
- pays recurring commission on qualifying paid revenue

This model is typically suitable for SEO agencies, consultants, web studios and companies that do not want to handle infrastructure billing themselves.

---

## 2. Agency / Wholesale Partner

The Partner may include SERVER1 infrastructure inside its own commercial offering.

SERVER1 may provide:

- partner pricing
- infrastructure
- provisioning
- technical support
- custom configurations
- API access for supported services and capabilities

The Partner may define:

- its own retail price
- its own margin
- its own client-facing package
- its own commercial presentation

Wholesale pricing is negotiated separately.

**Wholesale pricing is not automatically the same as Referral Commission.**

# Annual Qualifying Partner Revenue

Partner tier qualification is based on **Annual Qualifying Partner Revenue**.

This means the eligible net paid revenue attributed to the Partner during the applicable 12-month measurement period.

In practical terms:

> The qualifying revenue generated during the last 12 months by customers attributed to the Partner.

## Revenue that may qualify

Eligible revenue may include:

- Web Hosting
- WordPress Hosting
- VPS
- Managed VPS
- Unmanaged VPS
- Dedicated Server
- other qualifying recurring infrastructure services

## Revenue that does not normally qualify

The following are excluded unless separately agreed:

- VAT
- unpaid invoices
- refunded invoices
- cancelled invoices
- chargebacks
- free services
- promotional credits
- one-time setup fees
- one-time migration fees
- domain registrations unless separately defined
- third-party costs where commission does not apply

### Formula

```text
Annual Qualifying Partner Revenue =
eligible net paid revenue attributed to the Partner
during the applicable 12-month measurement period
```

---

# Commission Rules

Commission is calculated only on qualifying invoices that have actually been paid.

Commission is not generated on:

- unpaid invoices
- cancelled invoices
- refunded invoices
- chargebacks

If a commission has already been credited and the underlying customer payment is later refunded or charged back, SERVER1 may apply the corresponding adjustment to a future Partner settlement.

Recurring commission may continue while:

- the referred customer remains active
- the qualifying service remains active
- the invoice is actually paid
- the Partner relationship remains active under the applicable Partner Terms

# Partnership Philosophy

The SERVER1 Agency Partner Program is built around a simple long-term idea:

> Agencies should be able to focus on growth, development and the client relationship without having to build an internal infrastructure department for every project.

SERVER1 aims to become the infrastructure layer behind those agencies - helping with hosting, VPS, Dedicated Servers, migrations, infrastructure planning, supported API integrations and technical coordination.

The result should be a partnership where both sides can grow:

**Agency -> SERVER1 infrastructure opportunities**

with clear attribution, recurring economics, client protection and a non-exclusive commercial model.
